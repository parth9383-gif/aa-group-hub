Our story

From one restaurant to a group.

Our story starts in 2004, when founder Atiq Ahmad arrived in Canada and opened his first Popeyes restaurant within a year. What began as a single location grew into one of the largest Popeyes franchise operations in Ontario and Saskatchewan.

That growth was never for its own sake. In 2018, the family brought its businesses together as AA Group of Companies — a holding company that keeps one standard across every industry it touches.

Today the group operates five businesses: Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen franchise restaurants, AA Investment Group, AA Commodity Group, AA Travel & Tourism, and Ontario Cardio Imaging. Different industries, the same promise — we show up, we do the work, and we take care of the people who trust us.
