Issue 1 · Structure

No clear group structure online.

Before

One flat site, five businesses

One structure, one voice

  • One divisions URL; 301 the duplicate

Voice & compliance

What we say. What we never say.

We say

  • Family-led, professional, plain Canadian English (-ize)
  • "We build the plan around the people"
  • Facts with sources; [CONFIRM] where the client must verify
  • One CTA per post, low-pressure

We never say

  • Best · #1 · guaranteed · revolutionary
  • Return projections, IRRs, "invest now" (investment lane)
  • Patient testimonials or superiority claims (healthcare lane)
  • Price forecasts (commodity) · undated "limited time" (travel)
  • Invented numbers — ever

Who we sound like: a second-generation family owner who knows the file, has time for you, and does not oversell.
