Old website vs the new one

Side by side: what aagroupofcompanies.com does today, and what the new single-file hub does instead.

Old site measured 7 Nov 2026 · New site: aa-group-hub.html v4

Left column: the current WordPress site, as measured. Right column: the new hub, as built and verified.

Speed

The first thing a visitor feels, and the first thing search engines score.

Old site

3.27 seconds to load the homepage

WordPress, a general-purpose theme and its plugin stack, measured from a standard connection.

  • Homepage fully loaded in 3.27 s (our measurement, 7 Nov 2026)
  • Multiple theme and plugin requests on every page
  • Media served from the WordPress uploads folder, much of it full-size
New site

One file, about 0.9 MB in total

The entire hub, including gallery and team images, ships as a single HTML file with local media.

  • No WordPress, no plugin requests, no external theme assets
  • All media stored locally and sized for the page (~0.9 MB including the gallery)
  • Nothing to update, patch or cache-bust on the server side

Structure & SEO

How search engines and AI assistants read the site, not just how people do.

Old site

Thin crawl signals

The basics exist, but they are default plugin output rather than a deliberate structure.

  • robots.txt returns a 301 redirect — no proper crawl rules are served
  • Sitemap generated by All in One SEO; a thin index with little beyond page URLs
  • No FAQ structured data anywhere on the site
  • No Person or Book markup for the founder or the published book
New site

Seven clear sections with schema behind them

Home · Divisions · Founder & Book · Gallery · Leadership · FAQ · Contact — each a named tab, each machine-readable.

  • FAQPage schema on 10 question-and-answer pairs (written for answer-engine results)
  • Person + Book schema on the Founder & Book tab
  • Consistent headings and section IDs, so every tab is addressable and indexable
  • Print stylesheet included, so the same page doubles as a clean PDF

Facts & numbers

A group this size is judged on whether its own pages agree with each other.

Old site

Three facts, two versions each

Different pages were written at different times and never reconciled.

  • Restaurant count: 60+ on one page, 42 on another
  • Group revenue: $75M on one page, $50M on another
  • Copyright line: ©2018 in one footer, ©2025 in another
  • Contact page still carries template placeholder offices that are not the group's
New site

One set of numbers, used everywhere

We took the figures the group currently publishes as its latest and applied them across every tab.

  • 60+ restaurants — same figure on Home, Divisions and FAQ
  • $75M+ revenue — same figure on Home and FAQ
  • ©2026 in the single footer
  • Contact tab shows only the group's real contact details and a working form

Credibility & content

The proof the group already has, and whether the site shows it.

Old site

Real assets, mostly out of sight

The awards, the book and the team exist — the site just doesn't lead with them.

  • "Gallery" page shows only division logos; the awards photography is not there
  • Team portraits exist but are buried at /team-profile
  • No press or recognition strip on the homepage
  • No section for the founder's published book
  • No careers page at all
New site

The same assets, finally on the front page

Nothing here was invented — the portraits, awards photos and logos came from the old site. We rebuilt the frame around them.

  • Press & recognition strip on Home: TRIEC shortlist, Dubai book launch
  • Founder & Book tab: launch photo, buy links, Person + Book schema
  • Gallery rebuilt in year groups — 2020 · 2022 · 2023 · 2024 — with a lightbox
  • 9 named team portraits on the Leadership tab, not a sub-page
  • Home archive banner featuring the 2024 awards
  • Careers strip and a WhatsApp floating button for direct enquiries

Visual & feel

Whether the site looks like the group it represents.

Old site

A stock theme doing its best

Layout and type are the theme's defaults; the group's identity is applied on top rather than built in.

  • Generic WordPress theme structure and typography
  • Logos as the main visual content; little photography above the fold
  • Mixed copyright years and template leftovers give an unmaintained impression
New site

Dark ink and gold, built for the group

A deliberate palette and type system, applied consistently from the first pixel to the print view.

  • Dark ink header, gold accents, Cormorant Garamond headings, Manrope body
  • Header stays dark on every tab — verified in Chrome
  • Soft reveal animations on scroll, with full prefers-reduced-motion support
  • Rounded cards, soft shadows and generous spacing throughout

Mobile

Most first visits to a hospitality group happen on a phone.

Old site

Responsive by theme default

It fits the screen, but it carries the full desktop load and the same scattered content.

  • Same 3.27 s homepage load, with no mobile-specific trimming
  • Team and awards content remain several taps deep
  • No direct-message action for a visitor who just wants to ask a question
New site

Hamburger menu, one-tap contact

The seven tabs collapse into a working mobile menu; the gallery and lightbox are touch-friendly.

  • Mobile hamburger menu — tested and working in Chrome
  • WhatsApp float and contact form reachable from every tab
  • Lightweight single file, so the phone downloads the hub once and moves between tabs instantly
  • 0 JavaScript errors on load, desktop and mobile
