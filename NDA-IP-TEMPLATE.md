# NDA + IP CLAUSE — Dashava IT (client demo ke liye)

> ⚠️ Yeh ek **starting template** hai, legal advice nahi. Asli signed deal ke liye ek lawyer se review karwa lena. Lekin yeh structure + language turant usable hai.

---

## A. NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement) — short version

**Between:** Dashava IT ("Discloser") and the client/company named below ("Receiver").

**What is confidential:** saara work jo Dashava IT showcase karta hai — website designs, code, layouts, screenshots, videos, pricing, process, aur har cheez jo demo/presentation mein dikhayi jaye.

**Receiver ki zimmedari:**
1. Confidential info sirf **evaluate** karne ke liye use karega — copy, recreate, share, ya khud use karne ke liye **nahi**.
2. Screen recording, screenshots, "View Source", ya kisi bhi tarike se design/code **copy nahi** karega.
3. Info kisi third-party ko **share nahi** karega (employees/subbu ko bhi sirf "need to know" basis pe).

**Exceptions (yeh confidential NAHI hai):**
- Jo info pehle se public hai.
- Jo Receiver ne khud independently banaya (proof chahiye).
- Jo court/legal order se dena ho.

**Duration:** 2 saal (ya deal ho jaye to deal ke terms follow honge).

---

## B. IP Clause (Intellectual Property — sabse important)

1. **Ownership:** Saara design, code, assets (photos/logos jo Dashava banaye), aur creative work **Dashava IT ki property** hai — jab tak **full payment** clear nahi hota.
2. **Payment pe transfer:** Full payment clear hone ke **baad**, agreed deliverables ki ownership client ko transfer hoti hai.
3. **License before payment:** Payment se pehle client ko sirf **review/view** ka right hai — use, copy, launch, ya modify ka **nahi**.
4. **Reserved rights:** Dashava apne **portfolio** mein kaam dikha sakta hai (unless client alag likhwa le), aur reusable components/techniques ka right Dashava ke paas rehta hai.

## D. Kaise sign karwana hai (3 tareeke, best → basic)

| Level | Tarika | Legal strength | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Best** | **DocuSign / Adobe Sign / Eversign** — link bhejo, legally-binding e-signature | ⭐⭐⭐ Strongest | Free tier se start |
| **2. Better** | **Clickwrap gate** (humara `nda-gate.html`) — link kholte hi name + email + "I agree" + timestamp, phir demo unlock | ⭐⭐ Clickwrap (recognized) | Free (already built) |
| **3. Basic** | **Print + physical sign** — PDF bhejo, sign karke wapas | ⭐ Strong | Free |

**Recommendation:** pehle **clickwrap gate** (level 2) se turant start karo, aur formal deal pe **DocuSign** (level 1) use karo.
