Who we are

A family-led holding company, built to last.

We are AA Group of Companies, a family-led holding company based in Woodbridge, Ontario. We own and operate five businesses across food, investment, commodities, travel, and healthcare. Different industries, same promise: we show up, we do the work, and we take care of the people who trust us.

Our story starts in 2004, when our founder arrived in Canada and opened his first Popeyes restaurant within a year. That business grew across Ontario and Saskatchewan, and in 2018 it became AA Group of Companies.

Our Mission

We build and grow businesses that serve people well, create good jobs, and strengthen the communities we call home.

Our Vision

To be a family-led group known for doing right by our employees, our customers, and our partners, in every business we touch.

Our Outlook

Robust growth driven by strategic investment, innovation, and a commitment to operational excellence.

Our Values

FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Short, plain answers — written so search engines and AI assistants can lift them directly.

Where is AA Group of Companies headquartered?
AA Group of Companies is headquartered in Woodbridge, Ontario, Canada, with a commodity division office in Dubai.
What businesses does AA Group operate?
Five divisions: AA Investment Group, AA Travel & Tourism, AA Commodity Group, franchise restaurant operations (Popeyes Louisiana Kitchen stores), and the Ontario Cardio Imaging clinic in Pickering, Ontario.
How many locations does AA Group have?
60+ restaurant locations, as published on the group's divisions page.
Who founded AA Group of Companies?
Atiq Ahmad, who arrived in Canada in 2004 and opened his first Popeyes restaurant within a year. The group was founded in 2018. He is also the author of Thinking into Success.
Where is Ontario Cardio Imaging located?
1690 Desran Street, Unit 230, Pickering, ON L1V 0G9. Phone: 905-426-9555.
How do I contact a specific division?
Visit the Contact tab for direct numbers and emails, including the Dubai commodity office (+971 4 852 9242) and the Pickering clinic (905-426-9555).
What is Thinking into Success about?
Atiq Ahmad's book offers insights on entrepreneurship, leadership, and personal growth for aspiring entrepreneurs. It launched in Dubai on 22 February 2025; all proceeds support orphans.
Where can I buy the book?
On Amazon, the Gulf News online store, and at major bookstores including Kinokuniya.
Is AA Group hiring?
Yes — send your CV to contact@AAGroupOfCompanies.com. Openings span restaurants, healthcare, and corporate roles.
Does AA Investment Group accept new investors?
AA Investment Group serves its existing investors. Its content is for general information only and does not constitute an offer, solicitation, or investment advice.
