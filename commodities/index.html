About Us

The Name Which Means Trust and Transparency

We are proud to offer agri-commodities from various origins to any destination desired via our signature supply chain solutions with unwavering trust, professionalism, quality, and on-time delivery. We provide a full end-to-end solution in agri-commodities trade and development to our customers.

AA Commodities, based in the UAE as an autonomous entity backed by AA Group of Companies in Canada, builds on a proud legacy in agri-commodity trade. Our Process

General Operations & Process of Trade

01 · Cleaning & Sorting

The sourced product goes through mechanical stages of cleaning, processing, and bagging to achieve the required parameters of the contract with the buyer.

02 · Inland Transportation

We partner with credible transportation companies, ensuring fast, safe delivery with live GPS tracking and monitoring systems.

03 · Inventory & Storage

Effective handling procedures are put in place to achieve flexible and accurate management of inventory. Periodic fumigations are scheduled to ensure pest control.

04 · Forwarding & Documentation

Prompt shipments and quick documentation are crucial. We work with efficient forwarding agencies to guarantee timely processing and delivery of shipping documents.

05 · Initial Laboratory Analysis

We accept cargoes only if they pass our in-house parameter checks before transferring to third-party surveyors to double-check and authenticate quality.

06 · Independent Quality Survey

We engage independent Inspection Agencies and Surveyors to certify our products and conform to International Quality Standards before shipping.

07 · Shipping

We not only maintain excellent relationships with major international shipping lines but also engage with professional CHAs at origins, resulting in competitive freight and fast transactions.

08 · Market Updates

Regular harvest, pricing, and origin market intelligence shared directly with our trade partners so you can plan procurement with clarity.
